PORTRAIT COCKPIT

A mockup of STOMPY held upright. The window is square. The instruments sit above it. The controls sit below, where your thumbs are.

Fake world, real feel: walk, turn, aim and shoot the targets. Hold the phone upright to try it.

Every control sits in one band at the bottom, so both thumbs rest at the same height. LEGS on the left, AIM on the right, the fire buttons between.

No JUMP or PUNCH buttons. Tap LEGS, then tap and hold it: the jets burn. Double tap AIM: punch. Hold a fire button and slide it: you aim while you shoot.

AIM

Fire buttons only: no AIM stick. Bigger buttons in the right corner; slide a held one to aim, double tap the window to punch.

THUMB REACH

On a desktop: W/S throttle, A/D turn, drag the window or a held fire button to aim, 1 2 3 4 fire, space jumps, F punches, T targets.
