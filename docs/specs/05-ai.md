## Summary

Replace the single `think()` with a small behaviour library and a per-chassis
profile: how far it wants to be, whether it uses jump jets, when it retreats
to cool, what it targets, how it uses terrain. Add a difficulty setting that
scales skill, not stats. The goal is that each chassis fights recognisably
differently, and that HARD is tense rather than spongy.

## Design

### Perception

Before #16 an enemy became aware within 600 m or after 25 s; both are gone. The rules:

- Awareness by sight: line of sight (`rayTerrain`) to the player within
  `sight` m (per difficulty and weather), or being hit, or a wave spawn, or an
  ally within 200 m becoming aware (`shout`, with a 1 s delay).
- Lost contact: no line of sight for 8 s → return to `search` around the
  last known position before patrolling again.
- The AI aims at where it believes the player is: last seen position plus
  velocity extrapolation, refreshed only with line of sight. This stops
  enemies tracking through hills.

### Target selection

`pickTarget(e, game)`: the player by default; in escorts, convoy vehicles get
a weight (`mission.ai.prefer`); in co-op, the nearest or most damaged pilot by
a per-chassis preference (skirmishers pick the most damaged, heavies the
nearest). Re-evaluated every 2 s.

### Movement behaviours

Behaviours are functions `(e, ctx, dt) → { moveYaw, throttle, jets }`,
composed by the profile:

- `keepRange(pref, band)`: today's approach/back-off/strafe, kept.
- `useCover`: when heat > 60 or torso < 40%, pick the nearest point within
  120 m from which `rayTerrain` to the target is blocked (sample 8 bearings
  × 3 distances) and walk there; leave cover when heat < 35.
- `ridge`: for snipers, prefer positions 15 m+ above the target with line of
  sight (sample the terrain grid in a 300 m ring every 4 s).
- `harass`: light mechs orbit at 140–200 m, reverse strafe direction when hit,
  and jump over the player when within 60 m (jets for 1.2 s, land behind).
- `holdLine`: heavies advance slowly together; speed limited to the slowest
  of the group within 150 m.
- `avoidEdge` and `avoidAllies` (the collision pass already separates; this
  steers earlier).
- `brawler`: close to melee reach along the shortest line, punch when in
  reach and facing, keep punching a shut-down target; see
  [12-melee.md](12-melee.md) for the punch decision and reach avoidance
  that `harass` and `ridge` use.

### Fire discipline

Keep the laser burst-and-wander rule. Add:

- `heatCap` per difficulty (EASY 60, NORMAL 72, HARD 85) and a cool-down
  retreat below it.
- Alpha when the target's torso is under 25% and heat allows.
- Section targeting: HARD aims at the most damaged section's offset (left or
  right arm bias), NORMAL at centre, EASY with the current wandering error
  plus 30%.
- Missiles only with lock (facing within 0.3 rad for 1 s), never under 120 m.
- Autocannon lead uses the target's actual velocity (today's code; keep).
- Punch when in reach and facing, by difficulty (see 12-melee.md); close in
  on a shut-down target to do it.

### Group behaviour

Enemies within 200 m of each other form a group. Groups share awareness,
focus the same target, and the lightest member flanks (adds ±90° to its
approach bearing). That is the whole "tactics" layer; it reads as coordination
without pathfinding.

### Difficulty

Setting in SETTINGS and on the Free Play panel: EASY / NORMAL / HARD, stored
in `diff`. Scales: sight distance (400 / 600 / 800 m), base aim error
multiplier (1.4 / 1.0 / 0.7), heat cap, reaction delay after sighting (1.5 /
0.8 / 0.3 s), wave size (−1 / 0 / +1 where the mission allows), and whether
section targeting is on. Never HP, damage or speed.

### Debug view

With `?debug=1`, draw each enemy's state name and its believed target
position as HUD text; a line to its cover point. Essential for tuning.

## Code touchpoints

- `src/sim/ai.js`: `think(e, game, dt, fx)` becomes the orchestrator;
  `src/sim/ai/perception.js`, `behaviours.js`, `profiles.js`, `fire.js`.
- `src/data/chassis.js`: `ai: { profile: 'harass', pref, sight }` replaces
  `pref` and `acc0`.
- `src/sim/update.js`: group pass before `think`.
- `src/ui/menu.js`: difficulty picker; `store` key `diff`.
- `src/render/hud.js`: debug overlay.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A JACKAL on NORMAL cannot track the player through a hill (test: place a
   ridge between; its believed position stays at last-seen).
2. Heavies within 150 m move at the slowest member's speed (test).
3. A WARDEN at 85 heat on NORMAL walks to a point without line of sight and
   waits until under 35 (test with a scripted hill).
4. Over 60 s of a 4-enemy fight on each difficulty, the AI never exceeds its
   heat cap by its own fire (test).
5. Playtest: HARD mission 4 won within five attempts by the reviewer; EASY
   mission 4 won first try; testers correctly name the chassis from behaviour
   in a blind test (menu hidden) at least 4 of 5 times.
6. Debug overlay shows states and cover lines.

## Tests

`ai.test.js` for the above plus: unaware patrol stays inside BOUND; harass
reverses strafe within 0.5 s of being hit; snipers relocate after three
shots; missiles never fired under 120 m; alpha only below the torso threshold.

## Performance

`useCover` and `ridge` sampling do up to 24 and 48 `rayTerrain` calls on
their decision ticks (every 1 to 4 s per mech), not per frame. Stagger
decision ticks by mech index so they do not coincide. Budget: under 0.3 ms of
`update` per frame for eight enemies on the reference phone.
